Can I visit more than one dispensary per day?

Yes, in New York, adults can visit multiple marijuana dispensaries in a single day. However, you cannot exceed the legal purchase limits for cannabis products within a 24-hour period. For more details, see the state guidelines.

Can I bring cannabis across state borders?

No, it is illegal to transport cannabis across state lines, even if both states have legalized marijuana. Cannabis purchased at a New York cannabis dispensary must be consumed within the state. Learn more at the New York State Office of Cannabis Management.
