Other Reasons to Visit Our Dispensary

East Greenbush and the surrounding areas offer a mix of local flavor and outdoor access that makes the short drive to Albany worthwhile:

If you’d prefer not to drive, rideshare services like Uber and Lyft operate throughout the East Greenbush and Albany areas. Plan your ride in advance, especially during evening hours, and always consume responsibly—never drive after using cannabis products.

Crailo State Historic Site (Fort Crailo): This Dutch colonial manor dates to the early 1700s and offers exhibits on regional history and architecture. The grounds provide a peaceful setting for a brief historical detour.

East Greenbush Town Park: A community green space with walking paths, sports fields, and picnic areas. It’s a convenient spot to stretch your legs or enjoy the outdoors close to home.

Schodack Island State Park: Located along the Hudson River, this park features trails, bird-watching opportunities, and river access. Pack layers and plan for a few hours if you’re combining your dispensary trip with nature time.

Junior’s Bar & Grill: A neighborhood pub offering American fare, cold drinks, and a laid-back vibe. It’s an easy stop for a meal or snack on your way back through East Greenbush.

Places to Stay Nearby

Hampton Inn & Suites Albany-East Greenbush: A modern hotel with clean rooms and complimentary breakfast, located just off I-90. It’s a practical base if you’re visiting for more than a day.

Residence Inn by Marriott Albany East Greenbush/Tech Valley: Extended-stay suites with kitchenettes and separate living areas, ideal for longer visits. The property includes a fitness center and evening social hours.

Fairfield Inn & Suites Albany East Greenbush: Reliable mid-tier lodging with comfortable bedding and workspace areas. It’s close to dining options and highway access for easy navigation.

Holiday Inn Express & Suites East Greenbush (Albany-Skyline) by IHG: A straightforward option with free breakfast and indoor pool access. The location offers quick access to both East Greenbush and Albany attractions.

Americas Best Value Inn Albany East Greenbush: Budget-friendly accommodations with basic amenities and convenient parking. It’s a solid choice if you’re prioritizing affordability and proximity to I-90.
