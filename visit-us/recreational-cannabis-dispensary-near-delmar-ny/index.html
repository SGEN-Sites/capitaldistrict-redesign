Is weed legal in Delmar?

Great news, cannabis fans: both recreational and medicinal weed are legal within the state of New York, and that includes Delmar. That being said, it’s important to stay within the confines of New York state law in order to legally enjoy weed. You’re permitted to smoke weed in public in New York as long as you stay within areas where you’re also permitted to smoke tobacco (an important exception: you can’t smoke weed in a car). If you’re staying in a hotel or AirBnB, it’s smart to talk with the owner or property manager to ensure that they’re OK with cannabis use on their property.
