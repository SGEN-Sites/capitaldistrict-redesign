Is weed legal in Menands?

Both recreational and medicinal weed are legal throughout the state of New York, including in the village of Menands. That being said, it’s important that you don’t transport weed across state lines, as doing so is still considered a federal crime. You’re permitted to smoke weed outdoors in New York anywhere that you’re permitted to smoke tobacco.
