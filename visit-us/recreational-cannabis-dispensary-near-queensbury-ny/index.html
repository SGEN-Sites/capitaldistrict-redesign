Is weed legal in Queensbury?

Good news: weed is legal in New York, including in the town of Queensbury. Unlike many states, you’re also permitted to smoke weed in public in NY, as long as you’re in an area where tobacco use is also permitted. There are a few exceptions to this rule, including motor vehicles—you aren’t allowed to use cannabis in any motor vehicles, even if the vehicle is parked. You’ll need to be at least 21 years old to purchase cannabis. While weed is legal in most of the states surrounding New York, it’s still illegal to transport cannabis across state lines, as weed has yet to be legalized on a federal level.
