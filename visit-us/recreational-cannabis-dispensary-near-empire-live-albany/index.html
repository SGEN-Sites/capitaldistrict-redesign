Empire Live's Top Choice Dispensary

Looking for the Best Dispensary Near Empire Live Albany?

Empire Live is one of the best concert venues in Albany. It’s an intimate venue where you can enjoy shows by the hottest up-and-coming artists and have a great night with your friends. If you’re ready for your next show and are looking for flower and edibles close to the venue, stop by Capital District Cannabis for your perfect concert pairings.

Best Concert Cannabis Picks

Pregame your concert with some of the best weed in Albany. At Capital District cannabis, we have an impressive selection of discreet cannabis products for you to take on the go.

Edibles

Edibles are a crowd favorite for music lovers. They’re flavorful, discreet, and perfect for staying lifted through every encore at Empire Live.

Turn your water into a delicious, cannabis-infused refresher with the MyHi beverage sticks. The Boisterous Berry flavor carries 30mg of THC and a burst of caffeine to boost your energy before the show. They come in packs of three disposable sticks.

Bliss Drops by 1906 are swallowable “happy drops” that will give you a long-lasting feeling of bliss and happiness. These are vegan, calorie-free, sugar-free, and gluten-free, making them perfect for those who want the benefits of an edible without the additives. Enjoy your drops before you head out to your next show.

Milk Chocolate High Dose Mini Bars by Grön pack a punch with 100mg of THC. They’re made with rosin and cacao and are soy-free. They’re perfect for staying energized and sociable at after parties.

Pre-Rolls

Pre-rolls are an easy and shareable concert option. Light one up in a safe, designated area before heading in to enjoy the music.

Kick things off with 7 Seaz Apricot Kush Tidal Waves Triple Infused Pre-Rolls, which deliver a sweet apricot-honey flavor and a mellow, blissful high, perfect for a relaxed pre-show vibe.

For a more energizing boost, Rove’s Apple Fritter Infused Ice Pack delivers a powerful, fruity puff with an uplifting, social buzz to keep you dancing all night.

And if you’re looking for a straightforward, ready-to-enjoy option, Grassroots Nuclear Muffin Pre-Roll offers a balanced, euphoric high that complements live music perfectly.

Vape Cartridges

Our vape selections include some sleek and discreet designs in both disposable and rechargeable options.

Pink Rozay by Ayrloom is like sipping a chilled rosé by the pool. A sweet indica hybrid with delicious floral and strawberry notes, Pink Rozay will help you unwind and relax after a night of dancing and singing along to your favorite songs.

Rove’s Blue Dream Melted Diamond Live Resin vape satisfies your senses with vibrant berry flavors. This vape is ready to use, which means you won’t have to worry about charging or refilling. Unbox and enjoy.

Eureka’s Melon Madness/Sour Appleicious Slide disposable vape is for those who want their vapes to match their sophisticated music taste. You can switch between different flavors with just the flick of a button. Or place the switch in the middle to get a fruity burst of both flavors!
