Is weed legal in Rotterdam?

Yes, cannabis is legal for adult recreational use in New York State. You can learn more about New York’s cannabis laws by visiting the New York State Office of Cannabis Management. For local Rotterdam regulations, check the Town of Rotterdam’s official website. Always stay updated on the latest rules to ensure a safe and legal visit to any cannabis shop or weed store in the state.

Can I visit more than one dispensary per day?

Yes, in New York, adults 21 and over can visit multiple marijuana dispensaries in a single day. However, you cannot exceed the daily purchase limits set by state law. For more details, see the NY Cannabis FAQ.

Can I bring cannabis across state borders?

No, it is illegal to transport cannabis products across state lines, even if both states have legalized marijuana. Always consume your purchases within New York State. Learn more about these regulations here.

What do I need to buy weed?

To purchase cannabis at our cannabis dispensary, you must be at least 21 years old and present a valid government-issued photo ID. No medical card is required for recreational purchases. For more information, visit the NY Office of Cannabis Management.
