Is weed legal in Fort Hunter?

Yes, cannabis is legal for adult use in New York State, including Fort Hunter. Adults 21 and over can purchase and possess cannabis products from licensed marijuana dispensaries. For more information on New York’s cannabis laws, visit the New York State Office of Cannabis Management. For local regulations in Fort Hunter and Montgomery County, check the Montgomery County official website.

Can I visit more than one dispensary per day?

Yes, in New York, adults can visit multiple cannabis dispensaries in a single day. However, you must adhere to the daily purchase limits set by state law. Learn more about purchase limits here.

Can I bring cannabis across state borders?

No, it is illegal to transport cannabis across state lines, even if both states have legalized marijuana. Cannabis purchased at a New York marijuana dispensary must be consumed within the state. For more details, see the NY Cannabis FAQ.

What do I need to buy weed?

To purchase cannabis at our weed store, you must be at least 21 years old and present a valid government-issued photo ID. No medical card is required for recreational purchases. For more information, visit the NY Office of Cannabis Management.
