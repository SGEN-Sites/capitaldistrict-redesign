Is weed legal in Saratoga Springs?

Recreational and medicinal weed are both legal in New York, including in the city of Saratoga Springs. Unlike many states, New York state law allows you to smoke cannabis in public anywhere that you can smoke tobacco (with a few exceptions, including in both parked and moving motor vehicles). You need to be at least 21 years of age in order to smoke weed, and you’ll need to make your purchase from a licensed dispensary.

You’re permitted to have up to three ounces of cannabis on you in New York, as well as up to 24 grams of cannabis outside of your home (for personal use only). In your home, you may possess up to five pounds of weed.
