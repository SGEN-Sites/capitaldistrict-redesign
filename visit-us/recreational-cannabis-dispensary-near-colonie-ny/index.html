Other Reasons to Visit Our Dispensary From Colonie

Colonie sits right in the heart of the Capital Region, making the trip to our Central Avenue location effortless. You’re likely familiar with Wolf Road’s retail corridor and Washington Avenue’s dining scene. Our dispensary fits naturally into errands or weekend plans.

New York State Capitol Building & grounds: This stunning 19th-century Capitol features grand staircases, carved stonework, and free public tours. The surrounding Empire State Plaza offers green space and seasonal events worth exploring.

The Egg: An architecturally distinctive performing arts venue hosting concerts, theater, and cultural performances year-round. The Egg is currently closed for renovations but will reopen in January 2026.

Albany Pine Bush Preserve: A rare inland pine barrens ecosystem with miles of trails for hiking, bird-watching, and nature study. The preserve offers a quiet escape and educational programming for all ages.

USS Slater DE-766: A WWII destroyer escort museum docked on the Hudson River, offering guided tours and a tangible connection to naval history. It’s an engaging stop for history enthusiasts and families alike.

Schuyler Mansion State Historic Site: This elegant 18th-century mansion was home to Philip Schuyler, a Revolutionary War general and U.S. Senator. Especially for Hamilton musical fans, guided tours provide insight into colonial life and early American politics.
