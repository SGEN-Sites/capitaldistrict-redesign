Is weed legal in Latham?

In the state of New York including the town of Latham, both recreational and medicinal marijuana have been legalized. It’s important to understand New York state law in order to enjoy weed safely and legally. You’re allowed to smoke weed in public in the state of New York anywhere that you’re also permitted to smoke tobacco (although you are not allowed to smoke weed in a motor vehicle). If you’re staying in a hotel or an AirBnB, it’s a good idea to check with the owner or property manager to learn whether they’re OK with you using cannabis onto heir property.
