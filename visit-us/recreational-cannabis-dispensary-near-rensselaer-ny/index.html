FAQs About Buying Cannabis From Our Dispensary Near Rensselaer, NY

Is Weed Legal in Rensselaer, NY?

Yes, since 2021, all recreational marijuana is legal in New York, including cities like Rensselaer and Albany, where we’re located. As long as you have ID showing that you’re 21 or older, you can buy cannabis from us or any other licensed dispensary.

How much weed can I buy when I visit?

According to the law, individuals can purchase up to three ounces of weed flower and 24 grams of concentrates. Our team members can help you determine how much of each product you can buy.

Do I have to be a New York resident to buy weed?

No, anyone with a valid ID can buy cannabis, including those from out of state. However, it’s still illegal to transport cannabis across state lines, so if you are visiting from another state, you must use all your weed in New York first.

Can I smoke or vape onsite?

No, while the sale of cannabis is legal, using it in public places is not. Smoking, vaping, dabbing, and ingesting edibles onsite is illegal.

Other Reasons to Visit Capital District Cannabis and Wellness

While we’re not far from Rensselaer, we are in a nice part of Albany. We invite you to spend some time near our dispensary and check out some of the local hotspots like:

Westgate Pizza Bella – While Manhattan’s pizza gets all the attention, Albany has some incredible slices and pies, too. Westgate Pizza Bella is consistently well-rated for its selection and flavor. Plus, there’s a wide variety of traditional favorites and gourmet options, too.

Margarita City – If Mexican food is more your style, Margarita City is the place to be. This spot has some of the best tacos in Upstate, and they pair perfectly with an ice-cold margarita, either on the rocks or blended.
