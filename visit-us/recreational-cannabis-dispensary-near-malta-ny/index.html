Is weed legal in Malta?

In New York, cannabis consumption is legal for adults. For detailed information, refer to the state’s official resources here. Stay informed about local laws in Malta by checking the official town website here.

FAQs:
– You can purchase up to the legal limit of cannabis in one visit.
– Visiting multiple dispensaries in a day is allowed.
– Remember, it’s illegal to transport cannabis across state borders. Stay updated with our informative blogs.

How much cannabis can I buy in one visit?

In New York, recreational users can purchase up to 3 ounces of cannabis per day from a licensed dispensary.

Can I visit more than one dispensary per day?

Yes, you can visit multiple dispensaries in New York in a single day.

Can I bring cannabis across state borders?

No, it is illegal to transport cannabis across state lines, even if both states have legalized it.
