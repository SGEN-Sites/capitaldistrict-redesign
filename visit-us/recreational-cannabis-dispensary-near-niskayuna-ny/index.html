How much cannabis can I buy in one visit?

In New York, recreational cannabis users can purchase up to 3 ounces of cannabis per day from a licensed dispensary.

Can I visit more than one dispensary per day?

Yes, you can visit multiple dispensaries in a day as long as you adhere to the legal purchase limits at each location.

Can I bring cannabis across state borders?

It is illegal to transport cannabis across state borders, even if cannabis is legal in both states.
