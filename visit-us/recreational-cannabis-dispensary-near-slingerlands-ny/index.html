Is weed legal in Slingerlands?

Yes, cannabis is legal for adult use in New York State, including Slingerlands. Adults 21 and over can purchase and possess cannabis products from licensed marijuana dispensaries. For more information on New York’s cannabis laws, visit the New York State Office of Cannabis Management. For local regulations in Slingerlands and the Town of Bethlehem, check the Town of Bethlehem official website.

Can I visit more than one dispensary per day?

Yes, in New York, adults can visit multiple cannabis dispensaries in a single day. However, you cannot exceed the legal purchase limits set by the state. Learn more about purchase limits here.

Can I bring cannabis across state borders?

No, it is illegal to transport cannabis across state lines, even if both states have legalized cannabis. Always consume your products within New York State. For more details, see the NY Cannabis FAQ.

What do I need to buy weed?

To purchase cannabis at our marijuana dispensary, you must be at least 21 years old and present a valid government-issued photo ID. No medical card is required for recreational purchases. Read more about requirements here.
