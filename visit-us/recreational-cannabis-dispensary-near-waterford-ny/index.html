Is weed legal in Waterford?

Weed is legal throughout the state of New York, including in the village of Waterford. You’ll need to be at least 21 years old to make a purchase at a dispensary. While you’re allowed to consume cannabis in public throughout New York state anywhere that you can also consume tobacco, it’s against the law to consume weed in a motor vehicle, even if it’s parked. While weed is fully legal in New York and many surrounding states, it’s still illegal to transport weed across state lines, as it has yet to be legalized on a federal level.
