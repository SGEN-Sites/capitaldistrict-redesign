FAQs About Buying Cannabis From Our Dispensary Near Roessleville, NY

Is Weed Legal in Roessleville, NY?

Yes, recreational marijuana is legal in Roessleville and the rest of New York. Individuals just need a valid ID showing that they are at least 21 years old.

Do I have to live in New York to buy cannabis?

No, we love catering to out-of-state visitors. As long as you have a valid ID, you can buy cannabis from us. However, keep in mind that it’s illegal to transport weed across state lines. So, whatever you buy in New York has to stay in New York.

Do you have more than one dispensary location?

No, we just have one location right now. However, we hope to expand our dispensary network in the future! Our goal is to bring our top-tier cannabis products and exceptional customer service to more communities. Stay tuned for updates as we work towards bringing Capital District Cannabis and Wellness closer to you!

How much weed can I buy at once?

New York law states that individuals can buy up to three ounces of flower or 24 grams of concentrate. These limits are some of the highest in the country, as most other states (including those in New England) only allow one ounce of flower. Our team members can help you determine how much you can buy of individual products, too.
