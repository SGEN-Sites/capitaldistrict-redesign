What Is Our Weed Dispensary Near?

Capital District Cannabis & Wellness sits in the heart of Schenectady, a city that blends historic charm, cultural vibrancy, and a thriving local food scene. Our location makes it easy to pair your cannabis shopping trip with some of the best things to do in the Capital District. There’s something for every mood!

Proctors Theatre: Entertainment at Its Finest

A landmark in Schenectady since the 1920s, Proctors Theatre is just 8 minutes away from our dispensary. This beautifully restored performing arts center brings Broadway-caliber productions, live concerts, comedy shows, and indie films to the region. Whether you’re planning a date night or a fun evening with friends, Proctors offers world-class entertainment without leaving the Capital District.

Central Park Rose Garden: A Local Treasure for Relaxation

If nature and serenity are what you’re after, Schenectady’s Central Park Rose Garden is a must-visit. Just a short drive from our shop, this hidden gem features over 2,000 rose bushes that bloom throughout the season. The garden is not only a beautiful backdrop for a stroll, but also a quiet spot for reflection, photography, or even an afternoon picnic.

The Edison Downtown: Upscale Dining in the City Core

For a meal that feels both stylish and satisfying, head to The Edison Downtown. This popular dining spot is known for its contemporary American cuisine, craft cocktails, and chic atmosphere. It’s great for a celebratory dinner or simply a relaxing night out after a busy week.

Museum of Innovation and Science (miSci) – Curiosity for All Ages

Families and lifelong learners will love miSci, a museum dedicated to science, technology, and discovery. With interactive exhibits, hands-on activities, and an impressive planetarium, miSci is designed to spark curiosity for visitors of all ages. miSci is the perfect spot to spend the day. Take the kids for interactive learning or explore the exhibits at your own pace.

FAQs About Our Schenectady Dispensary

Is Capital District Cannabis and Wellness a medical or recreational dispensary?

Capital District Cannabis and Wellness is a fully recreational cannabis dispensary in Schenectady, NY, welcoming adults 21 and older with a valid ID. Whether you’re new to cannabis or a seasoned enthusiast, our knowledgeable budtenders are here to guide you through our wide selection of premium flower, edibles, concentrates, and more.

What forms of payment do you accept?

We are now accepting Cash, Debit, and Dutchie Pay.

Do I need an ID to purchase cannabis in New York?

Yes. All customers must be 21+ with a valid government-issued photo ID to shop at our dispensary.

Do you offer pickup or weed delivery?

Yes! Order online for pickup and free delivery today.

How much cannabis can I buy at a Schenectady dispensary near me?

Adults 21 + may purchase up to three ounces of flower or 24 grams of concentrates per day under New York law. Those limits apply across all dispensaries combined, so plan your shopping accordingly.

Will someone help me find the best cannabis products for my preferences?

Absolutely. Our trained budtenders listen to your goals—relief, creativity, sleep, flavor—and walk you through strains, potencies, and formats. We’ll match you with flower, tinctures, edibles, or dabs that fit your budget and tolerance, no guesswork required.

Does your dispensary have a loyalty program or specials?

We regularly offer deals and seasonal promotions, and our loyalty program rewards repeat shoppers with points and perks. Ask our team for details when you visit!
