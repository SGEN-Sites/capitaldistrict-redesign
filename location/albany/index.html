Where is Capital District Cannabis and Wellness Albany Dispensary located?

Capital District Cannabis & Wellness is conveniently located at 997 Central Ave in Albany, NY 12205, right on Central Avenue (NY-5) and just minutes from both I-87 and I-90. Is Capital District Cannabis and Wellness a medical or recreational dispensary?

Capital District Cannabis and Wellness Albany Dispensary is a fully recreational weed dispensary in Albany, welcoming adults 21 and older with a valid ID. Whether you’re new to cannabis or a seasoned enthusiast, our knowledgeable budtenders are here to guide you through our wide selection of premium flower, edibles, concentrates, and more.

When is Capital District Cannabis & Wellness open?

Looking for a dispensary open now in Albany? Capital District Cannabis and Wellness has convenient hours to fit your schedule! We’re open early and late Monday through Saturday from 9 AM to 9 PM, and on Sundays from 10 AM to 6 PM. Whether you’re starting your day or winding down, visit us at 997 Central Ave, Albany, NY, for all your cannabis needs.

What forms of payment do you accept?

We are now accepting Cash, Debit, and Dutchie Pay.

How much cannabis can I buy at an Albany dispensary?

Adults 21 + may purchase up to three ounces of flower or 24 grams of concentrates per day under New York law. Those limits apply across all dispensaries combined, so plan your shopping accordingly.

Can I shop for weed if I'm from out of state?

You can. Any adult 21 and up with a valid government ID can buy cannabis in New York, regardless of residency. Just remember it’s illegal to cross state lines with cannabis, so enjoy your purchases while you’re in the Empire State.
