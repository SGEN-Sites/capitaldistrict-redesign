Earn More Every Visit with Our Cannabis Rewards Program

Make every trip to Capital District Cannabis and Wellness go further. When you join our loyalty program, you’ll earn points on every purchase that can be redeemed for exclusive discounts, product perks, and members-only highlights. It’s our way of saying thank you for choosing us, whether you’re stopping in for your go-to favorites or exploring something new.

Signing up is quick, free, and starts working for you right away. From special promotions to early access on select drops, our rewards program is built to give regular shoppers more value over time. Ask a budtender in-store or sign up today to start earning.

Is there a loyalty or rewards program, and does it work across all locations?

Yes. Our rewards program is available at every Capital District Cannabis & Wellness store. Once you’re signed up, you can earn and redeem points at any of our locations—Albany, Clifton Park, or Schenectady.

Does your dispensary have a loyalty program or specials?

We regularly offer deals and seasonal promotions, and our loyalty program rewards repeat shoppers with points and perks. Ask our team for details when you visit!
