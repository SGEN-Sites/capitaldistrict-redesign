July 10 is when the cannabis concentrate community comes out! It’s the category-specific retail moment built around oils, extracts, and vape products, and Capital District Cannabis across all locations makes it worth planning around. Here’s what the day is about and how to make the most of your 710 shopping run.

What Is 710 Day?

“710” flipped upside down reads “OIL.” It’s shorthand for cannabis concentrates and oil-based extracts. The term circulated in online concentrate communities in the early 2010s as dabbing culture developed its own vocabulary: BHO, live resin, dab rigs, and the extraction formats that defined a rapidly growing product category. The holiday emerged organically from those communities and has since been adopted across dispensary marketing calendars nationally.

Where 4/20 celebrates cannabis broadly, 710 focuses on: vape cartridges, cannabis oils, live resin, rosin, wax, badder, shatter, and disposable vapes. It’s the day concentrate consumers plan their premium purchases around.

What Types of Concentrates Go on Sale for 710?

The extract category runs from approachable to artisanal. 

Wax and badder are the accessible ends, two textures at similar price points that are easy to portion and use without much setup. Wax has a drier, crumblier consistency. Badder folds and spreads like a smooth paste. 

Distillate goes a step further in processing, refining cannabis oil until only isolated cannabinoids remain at high, consistent potency. It’s terpene-light by design, built for consumers who prioritize clean, predictable effects over nuanced flavor. 

Vape cartridges are typically distillate-based and are the category’s most grab-and-go option, requiring nothing more than a compatible battery and a quick inhale. 

Live resin preserves the terpene profile of fresh-frozen plant material through solvent extraction, making it the flavor benchmark of the mid-to-upper tier and the first real taste of what cannabis can smell and taste like at its peak. 

Live rosin skips the solvents entirely. Heat, pressure, and ice water produce a concentrate prized for its extraction purity and unmatched by anything below it on the price spectrum.

How to Get the Most Out of a Dispensary 710 Sale

A few practical notes for making your 710 shopping run count:

  1. Compare concentrate types before committing.

Live resin for terpene-rich flavor, distillate for potency and consistency, rosin for solventless preference.

  1. Check unit pricing.

Cost per gram or per milligram of THC equivalent lets you compare across formats and price points. Bundle pricing on 710 often makes the higher-tier options accessible. The Capital District loyalty program lets you earn points on every purchase that can be redeemed for exclusive discounts, product perks, and members-only highlights.

  1. Plan ahead.

710 inventory in popular concentrate categories moves quickly during promotions. Shopping a day or two before July 10th gives you full selection access. 

  1. Store concentrates in a cool, dark environment.

Heat and light degrade both potency and terpene content faster than with flower.
