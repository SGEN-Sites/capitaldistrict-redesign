Spring in Upstate New York always feels like a reset. The snow melts, patios reopen, and the Capital Region starts to feel alive again. Right in the middle of that seasonal shift comes 4/20 — the unofficial holiday that brings cannabis consumers together across Albany, Schenectady, Clifton Park, and beyond.

Whether you’re planning a full-day celebration or just picking up something special for the evening, 4/20 is the perfect excuse to explore new cannabis products, revisit old favorites, and take advantage of some of the best weed deals of the year.

At Capital District Cannabis, all three locations are gearing up for a weekend that’s bigger, better, and more stocked than ever.

Start with the Essentials: Stocking Up on Upstate’s Best Cannabis for 4/20

Before the plans come together, the most important decision is what you’re bringing to the table.

Across our Albany, Schenectady, and Clifton Park dispensaries, you’ll find a curated menu of New York-grown flower, pre-rolls, edibles, concentrates, and vapes — all lab-tested and compliant with state regulations. Whether you prefer the ritual of rolling up, the convenience of a vape, or the longer-lasting experience of edibles, 4/20 is the time to try something new or grab extra of what you already love.

And yes — this is when the real savings show up.

4/20 typically means storewide promotions, bundle pricing, featured brand specials, and limited drops that don’t stick around long. If you’ve been waiting to explore higher-tier flower or stock up on pre-roll packs for the season ahead, this is the weekend to do it.

Keep an eye on updates from your nearest Capital District Cannabis location — Albany, Schenectady, or Clifton Park — as 4/20 deals are announced.

What to Do for 4/20 in the Capital Region

You don’t have to travel far to make the most of 4/20 in Upstate New York. The Capital District offers a little bit of everything, depending on how you like to celebrate.

If the weather cooperates, early spring walks through Washington Park in Albany or along the Mohawk River trails can set the tone for a laid-back afternoon. Clifton Park’s quieter neighborhoods and open spaces offer a slower pace, while Schenectady’s revitalized downtown makes for an easy dinner-and-drinks evening with friends.

Prefer something lower key? 4/20 has always been just as much about staying in as going out. A new strain, a favorite movie, a well-planned takeout order — sometimes that’s the perfect way to mark the day.

However you celebrate, always follow New York State guidelines and consume responsibly.

Pairing New York’s Best Cannabis with the Season

One of the simplest ways to elevate your 4/20 experience is by being intentional about what you choose.

Bright, citrus-forward strains tend to complement lighter spring meals and daytime plans. Earthier, gas-forward profiles pair well with richer comfort food and slower evenings. If you’re leaning social, you might look for something energizing and clear-headed. If your plan is to unwind, a more calming, body-forward option may fit better.

Not sure what fits your plans? The team at Capital District Cannabis in Albany, Schenectady, and Clifton Park can walk you through terpene profiles, potency levels, and product formats to help you dial in the right experience.

Celebrate 4/20 at Capital District Cannabis

With three convenient locations serving Albany, Schenectady, and Clifton Park, Capital District Cannabis makes it easy to celebrate locally without sacrificing quality or selection.

Whether you’re a longtime consumer or just exploring New York’s legal cannabis market for the first time, 4/20 is about enjoying the moment — responsibly, intentionally, and maybe with something new in your rotation.

Stop by your nearest dispensary location, explore the menu, and make this year’s 4/20 one worth remembering.
