THC-infused drinks, otherwise known as “cannabis drinks” or “weed beverages”, have become increasingly popular among cannabis users for their convenience, safety and delicious taste.

You can find these drinks in a wide range of beverages, they are very discreet to use, and can be bought here at Capital District Cannabis & Wellness.

Getting Started

Humans have been known to use cannabis for thousands of years, including in the form of drinks. Bhang, a combination of processed cannabis, milk and spices, is thought to have been the first drink made from the marijuana plant, and dates back to 1000 BC in India. Bhang was used to treat a range of ailments, such as as a pain reliever, sleep aid, lung congestion and to stimulate appetite.

Find Your Favorite

THC-infused drinks provide all the same benefits as other cannabis products. They can be a great alternative to alcohol for those looking for a buzz after a hard day, and can help make socializing more enjoyable and help you remember the entire evening.

Experience Something New

For those who are new to using cannabis, or have a lower tolerance, THC-infused drinks are a good option, as they usually contain around 5-10 mg of THC per serving. These drinks can help with pain relief, act as a sleep aid, reduce inflammation and boost your mood.

Drinking cannabis has also been found to be kinder to the body in the long run than smoking, which can lead to respiratory issues such as asthma, breathlessness etc. Many users of THC-infused drinks also appreciate that it is quite discreet, and that the high kicks in much faster than with edibles.
