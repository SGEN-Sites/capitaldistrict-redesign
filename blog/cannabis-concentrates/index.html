Cannabis concentrates can look a little intimidating the first time you shop for them. Wax, shatter, rosin, resin…it’s a lot of names for a category that’s actually pretty easy to understand once it’s explained.

That’s exactly what our cannabis concentrates guide is here to do. Let’s compare cannabis concentrates for beginners, answer your questions, and help you shop for the best cannabis concentrates at any of our NY dispensary locations. 

What Are Cannabis Concentrates?

Cannabis concentrates are made by extracting cannabinoids, terpenes, and other compounds from the cannabis plant. Concentrates ditch the plant matter and put what’s left into a more concentrated form. 

A lot of cannabis consumers choose cannabis extracts because they are more potent than flower and very rich in flavor, thanks to how the terpenes are preserved. Cannabis concentrate effects can be more intense with smaller amounts of product, too. One of the best things about concentrates is how versatile they are. You can smoke them or add them to your flower to boost the potency of your joint or bowl. 

Types of Concentrates: A Breakdown

It’s time for Weed Concentrates 101. When you shop the shelves for concentrates, you’ll see a lot of different types available. The names tell you about the texture of the concentrate as well as how it was produced. The most common THC concentrate types include: 

  • Wax: A soft, sticky cannabis concentrate with a scoopable texture.
  • Shatter: A firm, glass-like extract that can snap or break into pieces.
  • Badder: A whipped concentrate with a smooth, batter-like consistency.
  • Live resin: A concentrate made from fresh-frozen cannabis, often known for strong aroma and terpene preservation.
  • Live rosin: A solventless concentrate made with heat and pressure, often chosen by shoppers who care about plant-forward flavor.
  • Distillate: A refined cannabis oil commonly found in vape carts and infused cannabis products.
  • Hash: A classic concentrate made from collected trichomes, usually pressed or shaped into a workable form.

How to Smoke and Use Concentrates

Wondering how to smoke concentrates? There are a few methods, but not every method works for every product. If you aren’t sure what goes together, talk to one of our friendly budtenders for advice. 

Options for using concentrates include: 

  • Dab rig: A glass setup used to vaporize a small amount of concentrate on a heated surface. 
  • E-rig: The electronic version of a dab rig controls the heat without a torch. 
  • Dab pen: Portable device designed for small amounts of wax, badder, or similar concentrates. 
  • Vape cart: A cartridge filled with cannabis oil, usually paired with a battery.

THC concentrates can be strong, so pay attention to potency. Check the label, talk to the budtenders, and always start low and slow when learning how to use concentrates.

Rosin vs. Wax vs. Live Resin vs. Shatter: How to Choose

The wax vs shatter comparison comes down to two things: texture and handling. Wax is softer and easier to scoop. Shatter is harder and more brittle. Both can be potent. 

Live resin vs rosin is more about how the product is made. Live resin is usually made with solvents and fresh frozen cannabis, which preserves the terpenes of the plant. Rosin is solventless, using heat and pressure to create the concentrate instead. Both can have strong flavor and aroma, but rosin is often priced at a premium because of the process used to make it. 

So which cannabis concentrate products are best? It really depends. Some people care more about the flavor, some want the potency. You might prefer a specific texture depending on how you are using it, or you might need to stick to a certain price point. Talking to our budtenders can help you narrow down your options and find the right fit.
