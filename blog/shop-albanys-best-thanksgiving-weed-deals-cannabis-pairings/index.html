Picking your moment to shop for cannabis deals in Upstate New York can feel like choosing between two major holidays. Each November, Green Wednesday and Black Friday bookend Thanksgiving with the promise of discounts and exclusive drops, but how much you save and what you find can depend on which day you hit your local dispensary. Here’s how these two shopping events stack up for cannabis fans near Albany, Schenectady, and Clifton Park.

What Is Green Wednesday?

Green Wednesday falls on the Wednesday before Thanksgiving, landing on November 25 in 2026. It’s often compared to Black Friday in the broader retail world, but it’s focused entirely on cannabis. For many shoppers, Green Wednesday marks the unofficial kickoff to the holiday shopping season, with dispensaries announcing limited-time deals, product launches, and festive bundles designed for stocking up before the holiday rush.

How Does Black Friday Differ?

Black Friday, the day after Thanksgiving, falling on November 27 in 2026, remains the country’s biggest shopping holiday, and cannabis retailers have increasingly joined in. Dispensaries around Albany, Schenectady, and Clifton Park often offer a new round of savings on Black Friday, sometimes continuing deals from earlier in the week or revealing new bundle discounts for the long weekend.

The big distinction: You’ll sometimes see larger inventory blowouts, deeper discounts on popular categories, or special product drops timed just for Black Friday. Some stores might extend sales across the entire weekend. However, deal details are never exactly the same from year to year, and New York dispensaries set their own terms, from product limits to participating locations. Rest assured, you can expect the best deals at Capital Cannabis.

What Types of Deals Can You Expect?

While there’s no universal formula, common Green Wednesday and Black Friday cannabis deals in Upstate NY include:

– Discounted eighths or ounces of flower

– Mix-and-match promotions across pre-rolls and vapes

– Bulk edible offers or buy-one-get-one deals

– Loyalty point bonuses for repeat shoppers

– Giftable bundles or product samplers designed for the holidays

How to Decide When to Shop

Signing up for loyalty programs, like ours, is a smart way to get early notifications, or sometimes first dibs, on holiday deals as they’re announced. Learn more about loyalty enrollment if you want to stay ahead of midweek or Black Friday drops.

Holiday cannabis shopping in Upstate NY is about reading the landscape and timing your visit based on your own wishlist. For the latest deals, menus, and product updates as the season approaches, take a look at the our website as your go-to for local cannabis information.
