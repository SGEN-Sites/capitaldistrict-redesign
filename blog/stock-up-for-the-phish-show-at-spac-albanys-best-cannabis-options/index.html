Summer is the perfect time to catch a concert. Why not add Phish to your concert lineup? Phish is an American rock band that draws influence and inspiration from diverse genres, resulting in a unique sound that has captivated fans for decades. Their long-lived fame and dedication to their fan base are a testament to their talent and passion for the art. 

Phish will be performing at Broadview Stage at SPAC, Saratoga Springs, NY, on July 25, 26, 27. What better way to prepare and enjoy a night of good vibes and good music than with some of our products at Capital District Cannabis? We’re a conveniently located dispensary near SPAC, so you can stop by before the big day or take a quick detour to stock up on product favorites. 

Best Cannabis Picks for Jam Band Shows

Capital District has the best selection in stock to help elevate your concert experience. Check out some of our recommendations below.

Pre-rolls 

We offer a wide selection of premium pre-rolls for everyone to enjoy, regardless of their experience level.

Grab the Heady Tree Gas Leak Shorty’s Pre-Roll pack to share and get the party started. These pre-rolls pack a punch with a 41% level of THC and will energize and elevate you fast.

Pink Runtz by Sugarhouse Farms is a great option for those wanting to kick back, relax, and enjoy Phish’s greatest hits.

If you’re looking for something a little stronger, consider Rythm’s Blueberry Kush Remix Infused Pre-rolls. They bring a potent 41.5% level of THC wrapped in a potent, delicious flavor.

Blueberry Pancakes by Rec Roots is guaranteed to help you finish the night off feeling happy and deeply relaxed.

Vape carts 

Our vape carts are a discreet and easy option to elevate your jam band experience.

Lychee Dream by Ayrloom is an all-in-one, sativa, disposable cart. Its floral and fruity notes will inspire you to feel the music. We offer this product in 0.5g and 1g weights. 

Jaunty’s Headband 510 cart is a heavy hitter, serving you with 90% THC through creamy notes of lemon and diesel. You’ll experience a euphoric rush perfect for dancing like no one is watching.

Edibles

Treat yourself to some of our tasty edibles for a discreet and smooth experience.

MFNY’s Peach x Natty Dubs Live Resin Gummies are a delicious, vegan, indica option. These terpene-rich gummies come in a pack of 10, perfect for enjoying solo or with friends.

Stay hydrated and refreshed with Ayrloom’s infused beverages. Orange Creamsicle is an iconic summer flavor with a twist of THC that will clear your mind and energize you for the show.

Your Go-To Albany Dispensary for Concert Essentials

Visit us at Capital District Cannabis at 997 Central Ave Albany NY, 12205 to find your perfect concert pairings. Or you can browse our online menu and check out our pre-concert cannabis specials. Have an amazing time at the Phish shows!
