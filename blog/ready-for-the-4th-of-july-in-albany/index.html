Upstate New York does Independence Day right. Lakefront fireworks launched from barges in the middle of the southern basin at Lake George. The Firecracker4 road race moves through historic downtown Saratoga Springs at 8:15 AM before the city fully wakes up. The New York State 4th of July Celebration at Empire State Plaza in Albany, with fireworks bouncing off the Corning Tower and reflections in the plaza pools. Symphoria performing lakeside at Emerson Park on Owasco Lake as the sun goes down.

This is a region that knows how to mark the holiday. Here’s a guide to the best of it, and how to plan the day with the right cannabis selections from Capital District Cannabis.

Upstate NY: Here’s What’s Actually Happening

Albany draws upwards of 20,000 people to Empire State Plaza for the New York State Fourth of July Celebration. It’s the region’s largest fireworks show, with a festival that includes live music headliners, local food trucks, and pyrotechnics synchronized to the plaza’s midcentury modern architecture. Park at Albany’s public garages early and take the short walk in. The City Beer Hall’s All-American Throwback Brunch is a strong start to the day before heading to the plaza.

Saratoga Springs runs two distinct events worth planning around. The Celebrate Saratoga All-American Fireworks launches from the roof of the City Center Parking Garage, serving as the finale to a free downtown music festival that fills Caroline, Phila, and Ellsworth Jones streets with food and live bands. The Brown’s Beach Independence Day Fireworks over Saratoga Lake is a separate, classic waterfront display (details for the 2026 celebration to come). 

For the morning: the Firecracker4 4-mile road race through historic downtown Broadway is the longest and largest Independence Day road race in the Northeast, with the Sparkler Fun Run at 8:15 AM for families. The Saratoga July 4th Racing Festival at the historic Saratoga Race Course adds thoroughbred stakes racing to the holiday weekend.

Lake George launches fireworks directly from a barge in the southern basin. The crowds gather along Shepard Park beach, and the Lake George Steamboat Fireworks Cruise is an experience unto itself. Sailing the Adirondack waters aboard the historic Minne Ha-Ha paddlewheeler with a water-level view of the show. Bolton Landing hosts its own all-day festival at Rogers Memorial Park.

Other notable events: the Fireworks Over Boldt Castle in Alexandria Bay launch from Heart Island over the historic St. Lawrence River fortress. Symphoria Lakeside at Emerson Park on Owasco Lake brings the full Syracuse Symphoria orchestra lakeside for a patriotic concert. And Walkway Over the Hudson in Poughkeepsie offers a 212-foot elevated perspective over the mid-Hudson valley fireworks.

For adult-friendly evening options in Albany, the Official Albany 4th of July Bar Crawl through the Lark Street entertainment district starts at Bar Vegan at 5:00 PM with holiday drink specials. Goose performs with moe. at Albany Med Health System at SPAC.

10 Best Weed for Summer Parties in NY

  1. Florist Farms Sour Watermelon (Energize) Gummies: Bright watermelon gummies made with real fruit and organic ingredients, offering a clear-headed, uplifting edible experience with a tart, refreshing flavor.
  2. Ayrloom Lychee Dream Infused Pre-Roll: Juicy pear, sweet berry, and floral notes come together in this infused pre-roll pack with a bright, uplifting, and easygoing vibe. 
  3. Heady Tree Blue Nerdz Shorty’s Kief Infused Prerolls: Kief-infused pre-rolls with sweet candy-inspired flavor and a balanced mix of creative uplift and laid-back relaxation. 
  4. MFNY Pink Lemonade x Lemon Cane Live Resin Gummies: Pink lemonade gummies infused with terpene-rich live resin, blending citrusy sweetness with bright tropical and spicy flavor notes. 
  5. Plantwerks Redbullz Pre-roll: Convenient pre-roll pack offering a ready-to-use smoking option with a balanced, easy single-session experience. 
  6. Casa Verde Farms Florette Blue Dream Disposable: Pure resin Blue Dream vape delivering smooth, balanced hybrid effects with rich flavor and a convenient all-in-one design. 
  7. Grön Lychee Limeade Pearls: Exotic lychee and citrus-inspired pearls with a bright, uplifting sativa profile designed for an energized, focused daytime experience. 
  8. Cannabals Liquid Diamonds Sour Diesel AIO: High-potency liquid diamond vape with a classic sour diesel profile, designed for smooth, discreet, and consistent on-the-go use. 
  9. Hashtag Honey Strawberry Cough Pre-Roll: Convenient, ready-to-smoke pre-roll featuring Strawberry Cough with a smooth, uplifting profile ideal for an easy, on-the-go session. 
  10. Generic AF Blue Raspberry Gummies: Bright blue raspberry gummies with a bold, sweet flavor and a fun, evenly dosed edible experience.
