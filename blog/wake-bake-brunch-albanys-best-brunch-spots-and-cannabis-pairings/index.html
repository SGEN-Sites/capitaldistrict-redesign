Sometimes, the best time to get high is first thing in the morning to help you get a buzz and a boost to start your day. However, while toking on one of the best morning cannabis strains is enjoyable by itself, why not pair it with some delicious brunch?

Capital District Cannabis and Wellness is one of the best dispensaries in Albany, and we’re on a mission to help you wake and bake Albany the right way. How? By putting you onto the top breakfast places in Albany to pair with your morning weed. While you can’t exactly smoke and dine in public, these Albany brunch restaurants will help you get the day started on the right foot.

A Rundown of the Best Brunch Spots in Albany

If you haven’t indulged in Albany food and weed pairings before, now is the perfect time to realize your true potential. The first step is determining the best weed for brunch. Typically, Sativa-dominant strains will help give you a mental boost in the morning, and you may want something fruity and sweet to kick-start your tastebuds.

Once you have a strain or two ready to go, the next step is to know where to eat brunch in Albany. Here are some of our top choices:

  • City Line Bar and Grill – If you want a true brunch experience, City Line is one of the best spots in Albany. With a decadent menu and a lively brunch crowd, you’ll feel extra good after your meal.
  • Jack’s Diner – Sometimes, simplicity is the best option. That’s why we like homestyle restaurants for our weed and food pairing guide, and it doesn’t get much more homey than Jack’s Diner. This retro-style restaurant serves all your breakfast favorites in a simple, no-nonsense setting.
  • Capo’s Breakfast Spot – Capo’s is one of several breakfast-only restaurants in Albany, so it’s perfect for weekend brunch or a daily wake and bake routine. That said, if Capo’s doesn’t quite scratch the itch, there’s also The Breakfast Spot (unaffiliated) down the street.

A Quick Guide to Brunch and Cannabis Pairings

While knowing where to find a cannabis-friendly brunch is helpful, how can you know that you’re pairing the right weed with the right meal? As we mentioned, Sativa-dominant buds are the best cannabis strains for brunch because they energize you instead of relaxing you like Indica. However, don’t feel limited to just smoking a bowl before buying a breakfast bowl.

Alternatively, you can look at the best edibles for brunch so you can get high more discreetly while enjoying your meal. But keep in mind that edibles can take a couple of hours to kick in, so you might want to snack on one before heading to your favorite brunch spot. Look for edibles that aren’t too strong and aren’t too sweet. Brunch is all about combining sweet and savory, so let that help you decide which edibles to enjoy.

Take Advantage of the Brunch Flower Power Hour at Capital District Cannabis and Wellness

When looking up cannabis-friendly brunch ideas, you’ll start by searching for “brunch spots near me Albany.” Fortunately, Capital District Cannabis is centrally located to many of these restaurants, so it’s easy to get what you need before making your reservation. Better yet, we also offer the Brunch Flower Power Hour.

This power hour runs from 9 am to 11 am on weekends, and select cannabis products are featured during this time. So, we invite you to drop in on your way to brunch and see what options are on sale. Nothing feels better than discounted weed, especially when paired with a delicious meal. Happy brunching!
