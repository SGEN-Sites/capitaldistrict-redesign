Halloween in Albany is rich with community traditions. Albany bars gear up for the season with the Halloween Bar Crawl that brings together costumes, nightlife, and the crisp fall air. The crawl spans three nights, with the option to purchase a 3-day pass on a single ticket. That means you can join the full event or drop in when your schedule allows.

Albany Stops on the Halloween Bar Crawl Route

Albany’s Halloween Bar Crawl runs over select nights. Show up for all three or choose the evenings that fit your group plans. Tickets come with a free drink each evening as well as low-priced food and drink options when you flash your wristband. Of course, always drink responsibly as you indulge in the best drinks Albany has to offer. Participating bars include:

Bar Vegan

Lionheart Pub and Brewery

Bull and Bee Meadery

Savoy Taproom

Olde English Pub

Dive Upstate

Night 1 — Saturday, October 25, 2025

On October 25, Halloween Bar Crawl attendees can launch into the thrill of the season with the kickoff party. The event starts at 4:00 PM at Bar Vegan, where attendees can pick up their wristbands and get ready to hit the streets.

Night 2 — Friday, October 31, 2025

On Halloween night, the fun is ramping up, and there’s even more excitement in the air. The evening begins at 5:00 PM at Bar Vegan. After that, you can use your detailed digital pub crawl map to find your next stop. 

Night 3 — Saturday, November 1, 2025

November 1 is the Hangover Bar Crawl: the last chance to get out in costume and make the most of Albany’s nightlife before the spooky season fades. The event begins at Bar Vegan, where you can enjoy the after-party and celebrate the night away. 

Pair Your Night: Social, Mellow, or In-Between

Before you hit the Halloween Bar Crawl, stop by Capital District Cannabis at 997 Central Ave, Albany, NY 12205. Our budtenders can help you select cannabis products matched to your evening—relaxed social energy, quiet wind-down, or something discreet to carry along.

Stock up for the weekend with edibles that pair well with fall gatherings. Gron’s Pomegranate pearls will bring you to an elevated state of delight. And their Dark Chocolate Mini Bar, with an indica blend, helps you unwind as the night falls. 

Prefer a vape you can tuck away and use discreetly? Try fall-inspired flavors like Apple Jack by Rove or Honey Crisp cider by Ayrloom to keep the seasonal vibe going. We also carry pre-rolls and flower, so you can find the format that works best for you.

Our team is here to guide your selection. Let us know what kind of evening you’re planning—active bar-hopping, mellow hangouts, or a balanced mix—and we’ll match you with products that support the experience. Consume responsibly, plan your transportation ahead, and arrive prepared. (21+ only.)

Celebrate Halloween the Albany Way

Are you ready to take your Halloween celebration to the next level and enjoy it the Albany way? Head out for the Halloween Bar Crawl to see the best of what Albany’s nightlife has to offer. Don’t forget to stop by Capital District Cannabis to find the best cannabis pairing.
