Capital District Cannabis Partners with Folds of Honor

PartnersJun 23, 2025By Capital District1 min read

Shop the menu

Shopping Albany, NY

Shop now

At Capital District Cannabis, we believe in using our platform to make a meaningful difference—and this summer, we’re proud to partner with Folds of Honor, a nonprofit organization that provides life-changing scholarships to the spouses and children of fallen or disabled U.S. military service members and first responders.

Since 2007, Folds of Honor has awarded nearly 62,000 educational scholarships, helping to carry forward the legacies of those who made the ultimate sacrifice or returned home with life-altering injuries. Their mission is simple but powerful: Honor their sacrifice. Educate their legacy.

As a locally owned dispensary rooted in community values, we’re inviting our customers to join us in supporting this incredible cause. Whether you’re a veteran, have family in public service, or simply want to give back, every contribution helps open doors for families who deserve our support.

You can make a direct donation right here. Even the smallest gift goes a long way in helping a student pursue their goals and dreams.

Together, we can help bridge the gap between sacrifice and opportunity. Thank you for standing with us—and with Folds of Honor.
