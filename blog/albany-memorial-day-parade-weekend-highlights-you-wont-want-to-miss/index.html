Memorial Day festivities in the Capital City are a time-honored tradition, featuring the Memorial Day Parade that pays tribute to the brave service members who sacrificed for our country. Throughout the Memorial Day weekend, you’ll find countless things to do, from live music, exciting food, and drink experiences to some of the best Memorial Day weed deals, available at Capital District Cannabis and Wellness dispensaries.

Memorial Day Parades in Upstate NY

Memorial Day weekend across New York’s Capital Region is marked by a series of parades and ceremonies honoring those who have served. Communities in Albany, Schenectady, and Clifton Park typically host annual Memorial Day parades featuring local veterans’ groups, military representatives, and marching bands, all coming together in a shared show of respect and remembrance.

Many of these events are followed by wreath-laying ceremonies or moments of reflection, offering a meaningful way for residents and visitors alike to honor the holiday. As details are finalized each year, these parades remain a staple of the long weekend and a reflection of the region’s deep-rooted appreciation for those who served.

Things to Do Memorial Day Weekend

Here’s a peek at some of the things you can do this Memorial Day in Albany, Schenectady, and Clifton Park:

Tour the USS Slater

What better way to honor our military members than touring the iconic USS Slater, the country’s last WWII destroyer escort, still afloat? Meticulously restored to its original 1945 state, it’s one of 563 destroyers built during WWII. The USS Slater served in the Atlantic and Pacific Theaters before being decommissioned and sent to Greece in 1951, where it served the Hellenic Navy until 1991. Step aboard this National Historic Landmark for a guided tour!

Enjoy Sweet Sounds at Emack & Bolio’s

Nothing says summer like ice cream. To mark this Memorial Day weekend as the unofficial start of summer, why not have a cone while you enjoy local live music? Whether you want one of their famous hand-crafted ice cream cones, a bountiful cup, sundae, or their shoop-shoop’n shake, keep the sweet summer vibes going this Memorial Day Weekend with a trip to this Capitol City staple!

Stroll & Explore Downtown Schenectady

Spend part of your Memorial Day weekend wandering through Schenectady’s revitalized downtown, where history and local culture meet. From the historic streets around Proctors Theatre to nearby shops, cafés, and restaurants, it’s an easy place to spend a relaxed afternoon. Whether you’re grabbing a casual bite, catching live entertainment, or just enjoying the early summer weather, Schenectady offers a laid-back way to soak in the long weekend.

Walk the Trails at Vischer Ferry Nature Preserve

Looking to slow things down this Memorial Day weekend? Head over to Vischer Ferry Nature Preserve for a peaceful walk along scenic trails and historic canal paths. This local favorite offers a mix of wooded areas, open fields, and views along the old Erie Canal, making it a great spot for a relaxed afternoon outdoors. Whether you’re going for a solo walk, bringing your dog, or meeting up with friends, it’s an easy way to enjoy the start of summer without straying too far from town.

Host Your Own Backyard BBQ

Sometimes the best way to celebrate is by keeping things close to home. Fire up the grill, invite a few friends over, and lean into the unofficial start of summer with a backyard BBQ. Think burgers, fresh sides, and a relaxed afternoon that turns into a long evening outside.

To round out the vibe, consider stopping by Capital District Cannabis & Wellness beforehand to pick up something that fits the occasion, whether that’s easygoing edibles, pre-rolls for sharing, or a mellow strain to wind down the night. It’s a simple way to elevate the atmosphere and make the weekend feel just a little more intentional.
