Concert tickets in the Capital Region are an investment, and a little planning makes that investment pay off. Live music rewards preparation: knowing the venue, sizing up the crowd, and pairing the night with the right cannabis for live music can turn a good show into a great one. 

At Capital District Cannabis, we spend a lot of time matching products to moments, and a concert is one of the more fun moments to plan around. Here is our local guide to picking the right strain for three of the area’s favorite stages, Saratoga Performing Arts Center, MVP Arena, and Empire Live, plus a few picks to have on hand no matter the show. All recommendations are for adults 21 and older, and responsible use starts well before the opening act.

Choosing a Complementary Strain for Your Show 

Venue matters, and the genre on stage matters just as much. Matching your pick to the music is one of the simplest ways to land on the best cannabis strains for concerts, no matter where your seat is.

  • If live jazz is your genre of choice, keep a sativa hybrid on hand with relaxed, cerebral effects.
  • Rock music pairs well with a sativa strain, especially one carrying earthy, funky terpenes.
  • Classical music fans tend to enjoy a balanced hybrid with bright, lively terpenes.

7 Concert-Ready Strain Picks

This selection covers weed for concerts of nearly every kind, and each pick below ranks among our best strains for concerts this season.

Saratoga Performing Arts Center, or SPAC, is an open-air amphitheater set on the grounds of Saratoga Spa State Park. The venue seats 5,200 music lovers and hosts concerts across nearly every genre, from a relaxed outdoor evening to a lively, festival-style crowd.

Recommended strains:

  • MFNY Super Lemon Haze x Super Lemon Haze Live Resin Infused Pre-rolls are a sativa strain with 27% THC and a notable terpene profile. With four in a pack, it’s easy to share with friends before heading off to SPAC.
  • Cheech and Chong Legends Edible Mango is a great sativa option for anyone who prefers not to smoke. Edibles take longer to kick in, so plan on enjoying these about an hour before leaving. The bright mango flavor pairs with an uplifting, cerebral high that lasts through the show.

Located in Albany, MVP Arena is a configurable indoor arena that holds anywhere from 6,000 to 17,000 people. Smaller than a stadium, it’s still a mid-sized arena capable of hosting many nationally touring artists.

Recommended strains:

  • Breakfast Connections Trainwreck Blunt is a solid choice to prep for an evening at MVP Arena. With 26% THC and a terpene profile where beta caryophyllene takes the lead, it’s built to complement any event on the calendar.
  • Ayrloom Half +Half Singles is an upgraded take on an Arnold Palmer. It takes a little time to set in, but once it does, it stays with you for the whole night.

Empire Live is a modern, adaptable space that holds up to 1,000 people. It’s designed for good sightlines from most areas, with two hospitality stations on hand for patron comfort. This intimate, social setting calls for a balanced hybrid strain with bright terpenes.

Recommended strains:

  • Ruby Farms Limoncello “Classics” Pre-rolls have nearly 21% THC and bright, citrusy terpenes. They bring a classy, easygoing feel.
  • Ayrloom Blueberry Lavender “Pillow Talk” suits nights built around Led Zeppelin just as well as evenings spent unwinding to Mozart piano concertos.
  • High Peaks Margarita Can makes a far better pre-show sip than a traditional margarita on your way to Empire Live. The entourage effect is at work here, with balanced cannabinoid and terpene profiles built to leave you relaxed yet attentive.

Make a Stop at Capital District Cannabis Before the Show

Can’t make it into the shop before showtime? Order for pickup at a Capital District near you. The stages are calling. Come prepared and enjoy the show!
