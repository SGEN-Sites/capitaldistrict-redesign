Looking for something to help you mellow at the end of the day? Edibles can be an excellent cannabis format for the evening, and there are plenty of options to suit your flavor preferences and goals. 

We’re breaking down how edibles work, what you need to know about choosing the right product, and showing you which evening edibles are the most popular in Albany, Schenectady, and Clifton Park. 

How Edibles Differ From Other Formats for Evening Use

Edibles work differently from smoking or vaping because they have to pass through your digestive system before you’ll start to feel the effects. That means they can take longer to kick in, but the effects also typically last longer once they arrive. 

A slow build can be a big part of the appeal. You can take an edible after dinner and relax on the sofa. By the time the effects have fully developed, you are generally ready to get to sleep. Edibles are discreet, easy to dose, and simple to work into a nighttime routine.

Don’t assume that just because you aren’t feeling the effects, the edible isn’t working. It may just take a little longer. If you take more too soon, you could have a more intense experience than you intended.

Frequently Asked Questions

How long do edibles take to work?

Edibles can take 45 minutes to 2 hours to fully kick in. Some fast-acting products are designed for a shorter onset window, so read the label and give yourself plenty of time before deciding to take more. 

What should I do if the effects feel too strong? 

Stay calm, rest somewhere comfortable and quiet, and sip on some water. Strong edible effects will pass with time, and the more comfortable and calm you remain, the better you’ll feel. If you accidentally ingest edibles or feel unsafe, contact Poison Control or emergency services. 

How should I store leftover edibles? 

Keep edibles in their original packaging, clearly labeled, and store them out of reach of children and pets. Edibles can look like regular snacks, so secure storage is a must. Always read the packaging for more storage information (such as if refrigeration is recommended). 

What are the best strains for stress? Or the best cannabis strains for relaxation?

Many people looking for a mellow evening option tend to gravitate toward indica or indica-leaning strains. Classic relaxing weed strains include Granddaddy Purple, Northern Lights, and Wedding Cake. When it comes to edibles, though, the calming cannabis strains are only part of the overall picture. Cannabinoids like THC, CBD, and CBN also help shape the experience. 

A Note on Dosing

The best advice is the one you’ll hear again and again in the cannabis world: start low, go slow. 

When trying the best strains for anxiety and stress, start at the lowest recommended dose and then give yourself plenty of time to feel the full onset. Avoid stacking additional servings until you know how the product affects you, and don’t assume one product will produce the same results as another. Different strains, formats, and formulations can affect you differently.
