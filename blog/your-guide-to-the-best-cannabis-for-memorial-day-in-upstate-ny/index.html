The last Monday of May lands this year on May 25, 2026, and in Upstate New York, that means the season is officially open. From Saratoga Springs to the Finger Lakes, Memorial Day weekend kicks off summer with small-town parades, lakeside afternoons, VFW breakfasts, and backyard cookouts that stretch into the evening. It is a weekend about community, remembrance, and the long-awaited return of warm weather.

For adults 21 and older shopping for Memorial Day cannabis in the Capital District, this is also one of the busiest weekends of the year. Here’s what to know before you shop.

What Memorial Day in Upstate NY Looks Like

Communities across the region mark the holiday with parades featuring veterans, marching bands, and local fire departments. In Saratoga Springs, wreath-laying ceremonies take place at local memorials and town greens. Volunteer fire halls and VFW posts host community breakfasts. 

The weekend also signals opening day for Lake George and the broader Finger Lakes waterfront. Boating, fishing, and waterfront picnics are standard. Hiking in the Adirondack and Catskill Mountains picks back up, and farmers’ markets reopen for the season. It’s a full weekend, and planning around it makes a difference.

Best Cannabis for Memorial Day: Edibles and Ready-to-Share Options

  1. Empire State Chocolate Bar: Buttery toffee and pure, milk chocolatey joy that’s made with 100% pure cannabis oil
  2. Lemonade infused drink by Layup: Made with real lemon juice and natural cane sugar, it’s a perfectly balanced sip of tart and sweet
  3. Cherry Cola infused drink by High Peaks: Each sip blends the rich, classic taste of cola with the bright sweetness of cherry for a smooth, satisfying drink
  4. Strawberry Jam Live Rosin gummies by Jaunty: Crafted with 10mg THC, all-natural ingredients, and strain-specific live rosin
  5. Pear gummies by Wyld: 10mg THC and 10mg CBG per gummy, 10 gummies per container. 
  6. Hybrid Blend pre-roll pack by Anthem: .35g pre-rolls that are designed for occasional smokers
  7. Key Lime infused pre-roll pack by Ayrloom: Zesty flavors of a classic American pie-tart. Tangy balanced with a sweet finish
  8. Happy Camper disposable vape by Hudson Cannabis: Sweet with a hint of vanilla and the perfect flavor to roast while mellowing
  9. Apple Jack Melted Diamond Live Resin vaporizer by Rove: Delivers a mix of head and body effects, with a relaxing physical high balanced by a euphoric cerebral sensation
  10. Mango Haze all-in-one vape by Jaunty: Ideal for on-the-go use with a sweet mango aroma and flavor
