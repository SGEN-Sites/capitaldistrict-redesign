Curious about CBD but not sure where to start? We can help. CBD, or cannabidiol, has become a go-to option for many people thanks to its versatility and mellow nature. From simple CBD tinctures to CBD gummies, there is a formula and format to fit everyone’s routine. If you are wondering where to buy CBD, this guide will break down the basics so you can shop for a high-quality product that fits your needs.

CBD Types Explained: From Whole Plant to THC-Free

When you browse a CBD menu, you’ll see a lot of unfamiliar terms. Most of them have to do with how much of the hemp plant’s original profile is retained in the final product. 

Here’s what you need to know: 

  • Full spectrum CBD: Think “whole plant.” Full-spectrum products include CBD along with minor cannabinoids and natural hemp terpenes. They also contain trace THC from hemp-derived CBD (no more than 0.3%). Many shoppers choose full-spectrum products for a more rounded experience that feels true to the original plant. 
  • Broad spectrum CBD: This is similar to full spectrum, but minus the THC. You still get CBD plus the supporting cannabinoids and terpenes, but it’s marketed as THC-free CBD.
  • CBD isolate: Pure CBD, refined to remove other cannabinoids and terpenes. Isolate is flavor-neutral and easy to dose. It’s popular for unflavored CBD oils, capsules, and certain topicals that rely on consistency. 

Which product is best? It depends on your personal preferences. Our team can help you compare options side-by-side to find the best CBD products in NY for your routine and goals. 

The Benefits of CBD Oils and Tinctures for Everyday Wellness

CBD oils and CBD tinctures are the most flexible ways to incorporate CBD into your daily routine. You can use them by: 

  • Placing them under the tongue. Put the suggested dose beneath your tongue, hold it there for a few seconds, then swallow. Many customers like this method as it’s an easy-to-follow routine they can do almost anywhere. 
  • Mixing them with a beverage. You can add these unflavored products to smoothies, herbal teas, or even your morning coffee. 
  • Micro-dosing them. Use the dropper’s markings to take a small, consistent amount and adjust as needed. 

Fans of oils love the simple ingredients and portability. If you are new to CBD, a tincture with a clear milligram mark per serving can make tracking your dosing experiences simple. 

CBD Edibles and Gummies for Pre-Portioned Relief

CBD gummies are popular for two big reasons: taste and convenience. They come pre-portioned, often with 5-25 mg per piece, so it’s easy to know exactly how much you’re taking. 

Many shoppers looking for natural CBD relief for travel anxiety or an evening wind down keep gummies on hand because they are discreet and require zero prep. You’ll find different versions, including sugar-free and vegan options. Ask our team about our assorted packs, which allow you to try different strengths and flavors. 

CBD Topicals for Muscles and Joints 

If you need pain relief, this is the one for you. CBD topicals are designed for on-the-spot application. Many customers reach for them after a workout, during long days at their desks, or as a part of their nightly ritual. 

Look for labels that note the CBD content, the amount per application, and other ingredients. Since topicals are applied directly to the skin, they are ideal for targeted use when you want to avoid ingesting cannabinoids altogether. 

How to Choose Quality CBD in the Capital Region

No matter where you shop, whether it’s Albany, Schenectady, or anywhere nearby, focus on a few basics to find high-quality CBD. Look for recent third-party lab results (COAs) that confirm potency and support THC-free CBD claims for broad-spectrum and isolate varieties. 

Labels should clearly state total milligrams and be transparent about their sourcing and extraction methods. Pay attention to the other ingredients in the product as well. Topicals should have skin-friendly ingredients like shea butter or aloe, and gummies should use your preferred sweeteners and flavors. 

Always shop with a credible local CBD shop that can educate you on products and help you find the right one. A knowledgeable team at a CBD store in the Capital Region can help you compare options and choose something that suits your lifestyle.
