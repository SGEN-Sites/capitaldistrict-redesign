Leaf peeping in Upstate New York hits its stride each fall, as vibrant foliage transforms the highways and hilltops of the Adirondacks. For those who crave both unbeatable autumn views and a memorable way to experience them, the right cannabis strain pairs perfectly with this seasonal tradition. Here’s a guide to the best leaf-peeping near Albany and Schenectady with some recommended cannabis pairings.

Prime Adirondack Leaf Peeping Routes Near Albany

Some fall traditions are worth making time for, and the Capital Region is home to some of the Northeast’s most stunning foliage. Three standout routes promise dramatic color and diverse landscapes:

  • Prospect Mountain Veterans Memorial Highway: South of Lake George, this 5.5-mile route off Route 9 takes you to a 2,030-foot summit with panoramic, 100-mile views out to the Green Mountains and the High Peaks. The highway is typically open from late May to early November. Check for 2026-specific details before heading out.
  • Whiteface Veterans’ Memorial Highway: Near Wilmington, wind your way up eight miles of scenic roadway, gaining 3,300 feet in elevation. From the summit, explore a stone castle and a mountaintop café with breathtaking views. The highway operates daily through October 12, 2026, but verify hours before making the drive.
  • Central Adirondack Trail: This classic scenic byway stretches from Glens Falls to Rome, weaving past Lake George, forested hills, and the Adirondack Experience museum. Numerous pull-offs let you stop and soak in the reds and golds at your pace.

Timing your trip matters: Peak color comes fast and varies from year to year. The I LOVE NY weekly foliage report is the best way to track regional color, and a quick glance before you leave helps catch the most brilliant stretches of autumn.

Leaf Peeping and Cannabis: A Fall Tradition

For many, the ideal leaf-peeping day blends natural beauty with a relaxing cannabis pairing. Take a look at some of our suggestions below.

This year, the Animal House Apples & Bananas Preroll (hybrid, 1g) stands out as an option for relaxing into the cozy side of autumn at home. With a listed 28.68% THC, it’s something to enjoy after you’ve put away the keys and settled in, perhaps with the windows open to the lingering scent of turning leaves. Always check current availability online, as menus update frequently.

For those who prefer something to sip, the ayrloom Honeycrisp 2:1 drink (10mg THC : 5mg CBD) captures the taste of apple season and offers a laid-back hybrid profile for those riding passenger on the trip.

Getting the Most Out of Foliage Season in New York

If you’re new to exploring up north, don’t just follow the traffic to the most famous overlooks. Some of the best color emerges along quiet stretches of the Central Adirondack Trail or at smaller lakeside stops outside of peak hours. Local museums and small towns along the way, from Lake George to Inlet, add their own flavor to the trip, without the crowds.

Autumn in the Adirondacks is worth slowing down for, and a well-selected dispensary pick can help carry the season’s feeling long after you return. To explore cannabis options in the Capital Region, visit Capital District Cannabis & Wellness in Albany, Schenectady, or Clifton Park.
