Edibles are a great way to enjoy the effects of marijuana without smoking. Edibles have a much longer duration than inhaled marijuana, and can provide a more intense, longer-lasting high. Edibles can come in a variety of forms, including gummies, chocolates, cookies, hard candy, and more.

The Process

Edibles are made when marijuana is extracted from the plant and cooked with a fat like butter or oil, then mixed with ingredients like sugar and flour to make a food item. Edibles may also be made from tinctures, which are mixtures of marijuana extract and alcohol.

Getting Started

When it comes to consuming edibles, it’s important to start slow. The effects from edibles can take up to 2 hours to kick in, and can last for up to 8 hours. Edibles are also known for producing a stronger and longer-lasting high than smoking marijuana. Because of this, it’s important to be aware of how much you are consuming, and to wait at least 2 hours before consuming more.

Experience Something New

When purchasing edibles, make sure to read the label carefully. Many edibles are made with a wide variety of ingredients, including sugar, gluten, dairy, and artificial flavors and colors. Additionally, edibles may have added cannabinoids like THC or CBD, so be sure to read the label to know what you’re getting.

Edibles can be a great way to enjoy the effects of marijuana without smoking. They are convenient, discreet, and provide a stronger and longer-lasting high than smoking marijuana. Just make sure to read the label carefully and start slow, so you don’t over-indulge. Enjoy!
