Vapes come in many different shapes and sizes, but basically, they are all devices that vaporise cannabis rather than burning it. The main difference is in the heating element – when you smoke cannabis, it is heated to the point of combustion, which can cause some of the beneficial compounds to be destroyed.

On the other hand, when you vape cannabis flower or concentrate at a lower, more precise temperature (around 355 – 375 degrees Fahrenheit), the heat activates more cannabinoids and terpenes into a vapor, but stops short of combustion.

Getting Started

When using vape carts, it’s important to be mindful of the device you’re using. Vape carts are designed to be used with specific devices, so make sure you’re using the right device for the right cartridge. Additionally, make sure you’re keeping your vape cart clean and properly maintained, as this can help ensure that you’re getting the most out of your vape experience.

Find Your Favorite

When consuming vape carts, it’s also important to be aware of the potency of the product. Concentrates can contain up to 80% THC, making them much more potent than smoking marijuana. Additionally, the effects from concentrates tend to be more intense and longer lasting than smoking marijuana. Because of this, it’s important to be aware of how much you’re consuming and to start slow.

Experience Something New

When purchasing vape carts, make sure to read the label carefully. Many vape carts are made with a variety of ingredients, including sugar, gluten, dairy, and artificial flavors and colors. Additionally, vape carts may have added cannabinoids like CBD or CBN, so be sure to read the label to know what you’re getting.

Vape carts can be a great way to enjoy the effects of marijuana without smoking. They are convenient, discreet, and provide a more intense and immediate high than smoking marijuana. Just make sure to read the label carefully and start slow, so you don’t over-indulge. Enjoy!
