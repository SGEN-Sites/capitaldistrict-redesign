Thanksgiving and Friendsgiving mean one thing in Upstate New York: a gathering with enough flavor, laughter, and post-dinner relaxation to fill the short November days. For many, cannabis has become part of the table, both in the spirit of pairing with autumn favorites and bringing together friends looking for a festive experience. If you’re planning your own cozy celebration or looking for something to elevate your holiday routine, here are seven recommended cannabis picks currently available to shop in the Capital District.

1. Find | Bottomless Mints (Ounces) – Classic Flower for Sharing

The centerpiece of many get-togethers is classic flower. Find | Bottomless Mints (Ounces) by Grassroots offers a hybrid option with a robust 29.42% THC. It’s a one-ounce jar, perfect for those who want to keep the rolling tray available for a while or split among several friends. As the name hints, there’s a cool, dessert-like note that fits right in with Thanksgiving treats.

2. STIIIZY Blue Burst 40s Infused Pre-Roll – Simple, Social, and Potent

Pre-rolls are as convenient as it gets, especially when there are appetizers to prep. The STIIIZY Blue Burst 40s Infused Pre-Roll checks all the boxes for the friend who prefers to skip the grinding but doesn’t want to sacrifice strength, it’s a hybrid with a 36.33% THC infusion. With a flavor profile that suggests berry and blue candy, it feels right at home alongside cranberry sauce or a sweet potato side.

3. ayrloom Pink Rozay Infused Pre-Roll – One Pack, Five to Share

If you’ve got a larger group or want something to pass around when the desserts hit the table, consider the ayrloom Pink Rozay Infused Pre-Roll 5-pack. This indica option comes in at over 37% THC across its five 0.6g pre-rolls, enough to split among guests without anyone doubling back to the store mid-celebration. The name may sound like a toast, but it’s all cannabis flavor complexity inside.

4. ayrloom Apple Orchard Sunrise 2:1 – Autumn in Edible Form

Not everyone at the table wants to smoke, and plenty crave the nostalgia of holiday fruit. ayrloom Apple Orchard Sunrise 2:1 edibles bring an apple-forward, autumn-inspired twist to your dessert options. Each pack contains 10 pieces with both THC and CBG as listed on the menu (always check your label for exact amounts). These offer an easy route for those seeking a slower, more social edible format with fall flavor in every bite.

5. Nerd Bar Citrus Cooler AIO – A Sativa Vape for Energy

For gatherings that stretch from brunch through football and board games, a vape can provide another way to stay social without fuss. The Nerd Bar Citrus Cooler AIO brings a citrusy, light vibe and comes in an all-in-one (AIO) device with 85.11% THC sativa oil. Portable and easy to use, this fits the needs of guests who prefer a quick, discreet option or want to save room for pie.

Shop Cannabis For Thanksgiving in Albany

At Capital District Cannabis and Wellness, we keep our Albany menu updated for every season. Always check live inventory before shopping, as products and formats rotate often. You’ll find an evolving shelf of flower, pre-rolls, edibles, and vapes ready for every part of the gathering, whether it’s quiet afternoon prep or a full house of friends.
