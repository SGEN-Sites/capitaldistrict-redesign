If you’ve been waiting for the perfect time to stock up on weed at your local upstate dispensary, this is it. Capital District Cannabis & Wellness dispensaries are giving you the chance to turn your next cannabis purchase into a full-on concert experience.

For a limited time, customers who spend $100 or more in-store will be automatically entered to win tickets to one of the hottest R&B tours of the year. Whether you’re shopping for flower, pre-rolls, edibles, or vapes, your purchase could land you at an unforgettable live show.

Win Tickets to the R&B Tour at JMA Wireless Dome

This isn’t just any concert. It’s The R&B Tour: Raymond & Brown, featuring two of the biggest names in music, Usher and Chris Brown. The show is set for Saturday, August 1 at the JMA Wireless Dome, one of Upstate New York’s best concert venues.

From chart-topping hits like “Yeah!” and “U Got It Bad” to fan favorites like “Forever” and “No Guidance,” this tour brings a full-scale, high-energy production to Syracuse and you could be there just by doing your regular dispensary run. Whether you’re a longtime fan or just looking for a standout summer concert, this is one of the biggest live music events hitting the region.

Even better, winners of the ticket raffle will be announced over Memorial Day Weekend, giving you plenty of time to plan ahead for an unforgettable August show.

Shop Cannabis Near Albany, Clifton Park & Schenectady

No matter which location you visit, whether it’s our Albany, Clifton Park, or Schenectady dispensary, you’ll find a well-rounded menu packed with options for every preference and budget.

The lineup includes trusted New York brands like Ayrloom, Jaunty, Dime, Jetty, and Dragonfly, alongside a wide variety of products from premium flower with dozens of strains to choose from, to pre-rolls, vapes, concentrates, and infused options. Whether you’re looking to try something new or stock up on a go-to favorite, there’s no shortage of quality picks across categories and price points.

If you’re heading to the concert? It’s also a great time to think about convenience. Discreet options like edibles or low-dose infused products can be easy to bring along and enjoy without the need for extra accessories. Perfect for a packed venue and a high-energy night out.

How to Enter the Cannabis Concert Ticket Giveaway

Getting entered is simple:

Visit any Capital District Cannabis & Wellness dispensary (Albany, Clifton Park, Schenectady)

Spend $100 or more on cannabis products

Receive an automatic entry into the raffle

No extra steps, no complicated sign-ups. Just shop, enter, and you’re in the running.

Don’t Miss Your Chance to Pair Cannabis & Live Music

Opportunities like this don’t come around often. Whether you’re a longtime fan of Usher, can’t miss Chris Brown, or just love the idea of turning your dispensary visit into a concert night, this giveaway is worth jumping on.

Stop by Capital District Cannabis & Wellness to stock up on your favorite cannabis products, and get entered while you can.

Your next pickup could take you from the dispensary to the front row.
