The fun at the 2025 Altamont County Fair is set to begin soon! From August 12 – 17th, this annual event will be filled with the best food, entertainment, attractions, and exhibits in Altamont. While the exhibits and vendors may close at 10 PM, you can keep the good times rolling with us at Capital District Cannabis. Stop by our dispensary near Altament Fairgrounds, or place an online order for pickup before your fun-filled day to grab the perfect post-fair cannabis pairings.

Cannabis for Summer Fairs

Edibles to Satisfy Your Sweet Tooth

If the funnel cakes and fried Oreos weren’t enough to cure your sweet tooth, indulge in some of our edibles to end the night.

The ZONK! Strawberry Crunch Chocolate Bar is made with decadent white chocolate, strawberry flavoring, and crunchy puffed rice. It carries a total of 100mg of THC across 10 individual servings so that you can enjoy a balanced high solo or at a chill hangout with friends.

Crack open a High Peaks infused drink for a refreshing post-fair treat. Try their Strawberry Lemonade flavor for a tangy and sweet 10mg of THC and 5mg of CBD. This drink contains no sugar, calories, or carbs, making it the perfect option for those seeking a guilt-free treat!

Vape Carts for a Relaxing Post-Fair Wind Down

If you’re a fan of smokables but don’t want the fuss of a pre-roll, our vapes are a discreet alternative to a post-fair high.

The Fernway Berry Haze Traveler vape is a sleek and discreet indica vape. Its sweet berry flavor with earthy undertones delivers a deeply relaxing high, perfect to close out your jam-packed day.

PAX High Purity THC London Pound Cake vape is guaranteed to relax your body and your mind. This indica vape has a creamy vanilla taste with citrusy lemon notes. Made from ocean-bound plastic, this compact yet powerful vape is ideal for on-the-go adventures.

Pre-Rolls for the Perfect Nightcap

After a long day of navigating the carnival crowds and lines, chill out with some of our packed pre-rolls.

High Falls Canna pre-rolls offer a smooth smoking experience from start to finish. The Sativa Blend pre-rolls provide a happy and balanced high at a price you just can’t beat. Grab one (or two) of these pre-rolls next time you stop by our shop.

Leal pre-rolls bring you back to basics with a simple, dependable, packed pre-roll. Their Nic the Bruiser is a delicious indica-hybrid that will give you a relaxing body high and a happy and clear mind. 

How to Order Weed Near Altamont Fair

To make the very best out of your day at the Altamont County Fair, we recommend you grab your favorite products from our dispensary before you make your way to the fairgrounds. For a quick and easy trip, place your order online for pickup in our store. Make sure to bring your ID and enjoy your fresh cannabis responsibly!
