Shopping and ordering

5 questions
Do all of your locations offer the same menu?

All Capital District Cannabis & Wellness locations carry a similar selection of New York–licensed flower, edibles, vapes, concentrates, and other products, but inventory can vary by store. We recommend checking each location’s menu online to see what’s currently in stock before you stop in.

Can I place an online order and pick it up at any location?

Yes. You can pre-order online for any of our dispensaries—just make sure you select the correct pickup location at checkout. Your order will be prepared at the store you choose and held for the day of pickup.

Can I return or exchange products at any location?

Due to New York State regulations, cannabis products cannot be returned or exchanged once they’ve left the store unless there is a confirmed product defect. If you experience an issue, give us a call at the location where you made your purchase and our team will help resolve it.

Do you offer pickup or weed delivery?

Yes! Order online for pickup and free delivery today.

Will someone help me find the best cannabis products for my preferences?

Absolutely. Our trained budtenders listen to your goals—relief, creativity, sleep, flavor—and walk you through strains, potencies, and formats. We’ll match you with flower, tinctures, edibles, or dabs that fit your budget and tolerance, no guesswork required.

ID and age

3 questions
Is recreational cannabis legal in New York, and what do I need to shop?

Yes. Adults 21 and older can legally purchase recreational cannabis anywhere in New York State. You’ll need a valid government-issued ID—state-issued or out-of-state—to confirm your age before shopping.

Do I need an ID to shop at any of your locations?

Yes. New York State requires all adult-use dispensary customers to show a valid government-issued ID proving they are 21 or older. This applies at all Capital District Cannabis & Wellness locations, including Albany, Clifton Park, and Schenectady.

Do I need an ID to purchase cannabis in New York?

Yes. All customers must be 21+ with a valid government-issued photo ID to shop at our dispensary.

New York rules and limits

6 questions
Is Capital District Cannabis & Wellness a licensed New York dispensary?

Yes. Capital District Cannabis & Wellness is fully licensed by New York State to sell adult-use cannabis. That means every product we carry is sourced from state-regulated cultivators and manufacturers and meets New York’s testing and compliance standards.

What are the benefits of buying from a licensed New York dispensary versus unlicensed shops?

Licensed dispensaries are the only places where you can buy cannabis that has been lab-tested, tracked, and approved under New York’s regulatory system. This ensures accurate potency labeling, contaminant testing, and safe manufacturing practices. Unlicensed dispensaries are not regulated and may sell products of unknown origin or safety.

Do I have to be a NY resident to buy cannabis?

No. You do not need to live in New York to buy cannabis here. Any adult 21 or older—whether visiting from another state or traveling through Upstate NY—can legally purchase products at licensed dispensaries.

How much cannabis can adults purchase per day in New York State?

New York allows adults to purchase up to:

  • 3 ounces of flower, and
  • 24 grams of concentrates (including vape oils and dabs) per transaction.

These limits apply statewide and are the same across all licensed dispensaries.

How much cannabis can I buy at an Albany dispensary?

Adults 21 + may purchase up to three ounces of flower or 24 grams of concentrates per day under New York law. Those limits apply across all dispensaries combined, so plan your shopping accordingly.

Can I shop for weed if I'm from out of state?

You can. Any adult 21 and up with a valid government ID can buy cannabis in New York, regardless of residency. Just remember it’s illegal to cross state lines with cannabis, so enjoy your purchases while you’re in the Empire State.

Products and brands

8 questions
What types of cannabis products can I buy in New York?

New York’s legal cannabis market offers a wide range of options to choose from, including flower, pre-rolls, edibles, vape cartridges, concentrates, tinctures, beverages, and topicals. All products sold in licensed dispensaries are lab-tested for potency and safety, giving weed shoppers a consistent and reliable experience.

Where Can I Buy Ayrloom in New York?

Capital District Cannabis carries Ayrloom across all three locations in New York. Check the online menu for current availability by location. Capital District Cannabis has also run 15% off Ayrloom promotions, so it’s worth checking the specials page before your visit.

What Makes Ayrloom Different?

Ayrloom builds its products on over a century of beverage-making experience, and that flavor and formulation knowledge transfers directly into the cannabis drink line.

In the meantime, nano-emulsion technology is used across beverages and gummies for faster, more predictable onset compared to oil-based edibles. The Mood Series vapes take that functional approach further by pairing minor cannabinoids like CBN, CBG, and THCV with specific effect targets rather than relying on THC percentage alone.

Is Ayrloom Vegan?

Yes. Ayrloom gummies and beverages are vegan and gluten-free across the lineup. All products are lab-tested with Certificates of Analysis available via QR code on each package.

How Much Ayrloom Should I Take?

For THC drinks and gummies, the brand recommends starting with a small amount and waiting up to one hour before deciding whether to consume more. Nano-emulsion speeds the onset compared to traditional edibles, but effects still vary based on your metabolism and tolerance. For vapes, one or two draws followed by a few minutes of assessment is the standard starting approach.

Where can I buy Hashtag Honey in New York?

Hashtag Honey products are available at Capital District Cannabis locations across New York, including Albany, Clifton Park, and Schenectady. Stop by or check our online menu to see what’s currently in stock from this brand.

What makes Hashtag Honey different?

Hashtag Honey stands out for the sheer range of its catalog. Especially if you’re looking for a local cannabis brand, few New York brands offer this many strains across flower, vapes, pre-rolls, and gummies at once, and the brand keeps its lineup fresh with frequent strain rotations backed by lab testing for every batch.

How much Hashtag Honey should I take?

Dosing depends heavily on the product. When using Hashtag Honey gummies, start with a single piece and wait at least an hour before considering more. For vapes and pre-rolls, take it slow with short inhales and give yourself a few minutes between hits, especially when using the brand’s higher-THC strains.
