Capital District Cannabis & Wellness Halfmoon/Clifton Park Grand Opening

How it went down

The Rundown

Come celebrate and join us Saturday November 22nd for the official grand opening of our 3rd CDCW Location in Halfmoon/Clifton Park.

November 22nd 2025 at our brand new location located at 1508 US-9 Clifton Park NY 12065.

New spot, same crew, great vibes and as always the most fire products in the upstate area!

There will be raffles, giveaways, vendors and a food truck.

We can’t wait to see you there!
