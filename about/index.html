Our story

Come on in & explore

At Capital District Cannabis & Wellness we take pride in providing our customers with the highest quality products available. Our knowledgeable and friendly staff is always available to answer any questions you might have and help you find the perfect flower for your needs. So come on in and explore the world of cannabis with us!

Just getting started on your cannabis journey? Or maybe you’re just looking to expand your horizons. Whatever your experience, learn more about your favorite strains, categories, and in-store options with our learning material. Just choose a category to get started.

Our Purpose

A Different Experience

We believe in a unified cannabis marketplace—one that connects consumers, cultivators, and communities—and we’re proud to be part of that movement.

Locations

At Capital District Cannabis & Wellness, we’re committed to serving Upstate New York with trusted, state-licensed cannabis. As a New York–owned and operated dispensary, our goal is to bring high-quality flower, edibles, vapes, concentrates, and wellness-focused cannabis products to communities across the state. Whether you’re searching for a nearby recreational dispensary, exploring New York cannabis brands, or looking for guidance on strains, dosing, or product types, our team keeps the experience approachable and informed. We believe in a unified cannabis marketplace—one that connects consumers, cultivators, and communities—and we’re proud to be part of that movement. Visit us and see why cannabis shoppers throughout the region trust Capital District Cannabis & Wellness.
