How it works

Order Ahead

Side A: five steps from the couch to the counter

  1. A1

    Pick your store

    Albany, Clifton Park or Schenectady. Every Shop button on this site follows the store you pick.

  2. A2

    Browse by category

    Flower, Pre-Rolls, Edibles, Vapes, Concentrates, Tinctures, Beverages, Topicals, Accessories and CBD, each one a shelf on your store’s live menu.

  3. A3

    Add to your cart

    Add what you like. Your cart stays with the store you picked.

  4. A4

    Check out, choose pickup

    Check out on the live menu and choose pickup. Pre-orders close 30 minutes before closing, so 8:30 PM on a 9 PM night.

  5. A5

    Pick up with your 21+ ID

    Bring a valid government-issued photo ID that shows you are 21 or older. The stores accept Cash, Debit, and Dutchie Pay.

Any Questions? We've Got Answers

Ordering, pickup, payment and ID, straight from our store FAQs.

Pickup details
Can I place an online order and pick it up at any location?

Yes. You can pre-order online for any of our dispensaries—just make sure you select the correct pickup location at checkout. Your order will be prepared at the store you choose and held for the day of pickup.

Do all of your locations offer the same menu?

All Capital District Cannabis & Wellness locations carry a similar selection of New York–licensed flower, edibles, vapes, concentrates, and other products, but inventory can vary by store. We recommend checking each location’s menu online to see what’s currently in stock before you stop in.

What forms of payment do you accept?

We are now accepting Cash, Debit, and Dutchie Pay.

Do I need an ID to purchase cannabis in New York?

Yes. All customers must be 21+ with a valid government-issued photo ID to shop at our dispensary.

Can I shop for weed if I'm from out of state?

You can. Any adult 21 and up with a valid government ID can buy cannabis in New York, regardless of residency. Just remember it’s illegal to cross state lines with cannabis, so enjoy your purchases while you’re in the Empire State.
