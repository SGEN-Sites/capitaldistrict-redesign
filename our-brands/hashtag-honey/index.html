The story

Hashtag Honey is a New York cannabis brand known for its high-potency flower, vapes, pre-rolls, and gummies. The brand has built a following across the state for its wide strain variety and consistent lab-tested quality, and you can find it on the shelves right here at Capital District Cannabis.

Liner notes

Hashtag Honey Reviews

Consumer feedback on Hashtag Honey tends to center on value, with several New York cannabis consumers describing the brand’s vape carts and disposables as a strong option for the brand’s price point. Permanent Marker comes up repeatedly as a standout, with multiple users calling out its strong effects, smooth hardware, and reliable performance without clogging issues. Other strains like Strawberry Diesel, NYC Diesel, and Lemon Cherry Gelato also get positive mentions for delivering solid effects and decent flavor.

Several reviewers note that Hashtag Honey’s hardware holds up well over repeated use, with consistent hits and no burning or clogging even after going through multiple devices. For shoppers focused on getting good value without sacrificing quality, you’ll find this brand recommended frequently as a dependable everyday choice. Secret consumer tip: Look for RS-11 being in stock at your favorite dispensary as a strain worth trying.

The lineup

Hashtag Honey Cannabis Product Line

A1

Hashtag Honey offers one of the more expansive lineups you’ll find from a single New York brand. Their flower selection rotates through strains like Blue Dream and Unicorn Piss, available in both standard 3.5g jars and larger ounce options for those who go through flower quickly. On the vape side, the brand offers both 1g and 2g all-in-one disposables in strains ranging from Durban Poison to RS-11 and Strawberry Diesel.

A2

For smokers who prefer pre-rolls, Hashtag Honey has single tubes and 5-count tins in flavors like Jack Frost and Marshmallow OG. The edibles line includes gummies in flavors such as Strawberry, Green Apple, Tropical Punch, Blue Razz, and Watermelon, giving customers plenty of options for dosed treats. Concentrate fans can also find live sugar and rosin extracts in rotating strains like Lemon Cake and Blueberry Muffin. And of course, you can always check current availability at Capital District Cannabis.

Hashtag Honey FAQs

Where can I buy Hashtag Honey in New York?

Hashtag Honey products are available at Capital District Cannabis locations across New York, including Albany, Clifton Park, and Schenectady. Stop by or check our online menu to see what’s currently in stock from this brand.

What makes Hashtag Honey different?

Hashtag Honey stands out for the sheer range of its catalog. Especially if you’re looking for a local cannabis brand, few New York brands offer this many strains across flower, vapes, pre-rolls, and gummies at once, and the brand keeps its lineup fresh with frequent strain rotations backed by lab testing for every batch.

How much Hashtag Honey should I take?

Dosing depends heavily on the product. When using Hashtag Honey gummies, start with a single piece and wait at least an hour before considering more. For vapes and pre-rolls, take it slow with short inhales and give yourself a few minutes between hits, especially when using the brand’s higher-THC strains.
