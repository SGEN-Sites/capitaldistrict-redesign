The story

As a New York cannabis brand, Ayrloom is rooted in more than a century of agricultural tradition. In fact, it’s grown from the same family behind Beak & Skiff Apple Orchards and 1911 Established Hard Cider in Central New York. The Ayrloom product line spans beverages, vapes, gummies, pre-rolls, tinctures, and balms, with THC drinks as its flagship category. And of course, Ayrloom cannabis products are available at all Capital District Cannabis locations.

Liner notes

Ayrloom Cannabis Reviews

Ayrloom has earned a reputation as New York’s leading cannabis beverage brand, and the reviews back that up. A thorough drink-by-drink breakdown rated the Orange Creamsicle and Vanilla Cola as the top two flavors, praising their accuracy to classic sodas without carbonation or an herbal aftertaste. Half + Half and Yuzu Citrus were highlighted as lower-calorie options well-suited to social settings. Overall, Ayrloom shines as one of the more refined entries in the infused drink space, reflecting the brand’s food-and-beverage background.

On the vape side, reviewers emphasize a consistent onset, clean vapor, and accurate flavor. The Cereal Milk hybrid gets praise for its euphoric, balanced head-high with a relaxed body finish, while Lime Haze earns top marks for its fast-acting, energizing effect. The Mood Series, which uses cannabinoid blends like THC:CBN for rest and THC:THCV:CBG for energy, is noted as one of the more thoughtfully formulated functional vape lines available in New York.

The lineup

Ayrloom Cannabis Product Line

  1. A1

    Ayrloom THC Drinks

    Ayrloom THC Drinks are nano-emulsified beverages with a 10mg THC to 5mg CBD ratio per can, available in nine flavors: Honeycrisp, Cran-Apple, Black Cherry, Pineapple Mango, Yuzu Citrus, Half + Half, Vanilla Cola, Lemonade, and Orange Creamsicle. They’re fast-acting, vegan, and gluten-free.

  2. A2

    Ayrloom Pre-Rolls

    Ayrloom Pre-Rolls are crafted with house-grown flower and house-made extracts for a smooth, consistent burn that highlights each strain’s terpene profile.

  3. A3

    Ayrloom Vapes

    Ayrloom Vapes include strain-specific All-In-One disposables at 90% THC, as well as a Mood Series with functional cannabinoid blends: Energy (THC:THCV:CBG), Bliss (THC:CBC), Calm (THC:CBD), and Rest (THC:CBN).

  4. A4

    Ayrloom Gummies

    Ayrloom Gummies are fast-acting, vegan, and gluten-free, with mood-specific formulations such as Island Time, Pillow Talk, Orchard Sunrise, and Sunny Days.

  5. A5

    Tinctures and Balms

    Tinctures and Balms round out the lineup for smoke-free and topical consumption options.

Ayrloom FAQs

Where Can I Buy Ayrloom in New York?

Capital District Cannabis carries Ayrloom across all three locations in New York. Check the online menu for current availability by location. Capital District Cannabis has also run 15% off Ayrloom promotions, so it’s worth checking the specials page before your visit.

What Makes Ayrloom Different?

Ayrloom builds its products on over a century of beverage-making experience, and that flavor and formulation knowledge transfers directly into the cannabis drink line.

In the meantime, nano-emulsion technology is used across beverages and gummies for faster, more predictable onset compared to oil-based edibles. The Mood Series vapes take that functional approach further by pairing minor cannabinoids like CBN, CBG, and THCV with specific effect targets rather than relying on THC percentage alone.

Is Ayrloom Vegan?

Yes. Ayrloom gummies and beverages are vegan and gluten-free across the lineup. All products are lab-tested with Certificates of Analysis available via QR code on each package.

How Much Ayrloom Should I Take?

For THC drinks and gummies, the brand recommends starting with a small amount and waiting up to one hour before deciding whether to consume more. Nano-emulsion speeds the onset compared to traditional edibles, but effects still vary based on your metabolism and tolerance. For vapes, one or two draws followed by a few minutes of assessment is the standard starting approach.
