Pickup at the Counter

Ordered ahead? Here is what to bring and what happens when you pull up to Albany, Clifton Park or Schenectady.

Start an orderHow to order
21+ with ID

What to bring

Pocket Check

Two things in your pocket and the right address. That is the whole list.

Your 21+ ID

All customers must be 21+ with a valid government-issued photo ID to shop at our dispensary.

A way to pay

We are now accepting Cash, Debit, and Dutchie Pay.

The right store

You can pre-order online for any of our dispensaries—just make sure you select the correct pickup location at checkout. Your order will be prepared at the store you choose and held for the day of pickup.

At the counter

Side B Pickup

  1. B1

    Head to the store you picked

    Your order is prepared at the store you chose at checkout, so double-check the address below before you go.

  2. B2

    Show your ID at the counter

    A valid government-issued photo ID that shows you are 21 or older. New York requires it for every adult-use purchase.

  3. B3

    Pay your way

    All three stores accept Cash, Debit, and Dutchie Pay.

  4. B4

    Collect your order

    Your budtender hands it over. Questions about a product? Ask before you head out.

Know your limits

Per Day in New York

3 ozof flower

24 gof concentrates

Adults 21 + may purchase up to three ounces of flower or 24 grams of concentrates per day under New York law. Those limits apply across all dispensaries combined, so plan your shopping accordingly.

Any Questions? We've Got Answers

Pickup, ID, payment and returns, from our store FAQs.

Start an order
Can I place an online order and pick it up at any location?

Yes. You can pre-order online for any of our dispensaries—just make sure you select the correct pickup location at checkout. Your order will be prepared at the store you choose and held for the day of pickup.

Do I need an ID to purchase cannabis in New York?

Yes. All customers must be 21+ with a valid government-issued photo ID to shop at our dispensary.

What forms of payment do you accept?

We are now accepting Cash, Debit, and Dutchie Pay.

Do you offer pickup or weed delivery?

Yes! Order online for pickup and free delivery today.

Can I return or exchange products at any location?

Due to New York State regulations, cannabis products cannot be returned or exchanged once they’ve left the store unless there is a confirmed product defect. If you experience an issue, give us a call at the location where you made your purchase and our team will help resolve it.
