Capital District Cannabis & Wellness Privacy Policy

Effective Date: 12/8/23

This Privacy Policy outlines how Capital District Cannabis & Wellness (“we,” “us,” or “our”) collects, uses, and safeguards the personal information of visitors to our website. By accessing or using our website, you agree to the terms and practices described in this Privacy Policy.

Information We Collect

We collect personal information from website visitors through various means, including forms, cookies, and our marketing, e-commerce, and point of sale (POS) systems. The types of personal information we may collect include:

  • Name, email address, and contact information
  • Browsing behavior and interactions with our website
  • Information necessary to process orders and provide customer support

We collaborate with the following service providers to enhance our website functionality and offer a seamless user experience. For detailed information about their data collection practices, please refer to their respective privacy policies and terms of service:

  • AlpineIQ – https://lab.alpineiq.com/trackingPreferences/2007
  • Dutchie – Privacy Policy: dutchie.com/privacy | Terms and Conditions: dutchie.com/terms

For a comprehensive understanding of their data practices, we recommend reviewing their respective privacy policies and terms of service.

Purpose of Data Collection

We collect personal information to:

  • Process orders and provide customer support
  • Personalize the user experience on our website
  • Improve our products, services, and website functionality
  • Comply with legal obligations

Sharing of Personal Information

We do not share or sell your personal information with third parties for their own marketing purposes. However, we may share your information with trusted service providers who assist us in operating our business and providing our services, such as payment processors, analytics services, and our POS system provider.

Use of Cookies

We use cookies and similar technologies to enhance your browsing experience on our website. The cookies we use include:

  • Essential cookies: Necessary for the functioning of our website and enabling core features.
  • Analytics cookies: Used to gather statistical information about website usage and help us improve our services.

You can manage your cookie preferences through your browser settings. Please note that disabling cookies may impact the functionality and user experience of our website.

Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect the personal information we collect from unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, or destruction. Our partners have implemented security measures to safeguard your data. However, please note that no method of data transmission over the internet or electronic storage is entirely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Data Retention

We retain personal information for a period of 12 months or as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes outlined in this Privacy Policy, unless a longer retention period is required or permitted by law.

Rights of Website Visitors

You have the right to access, correct, update, and delete your personal information. To exercise these rights or make inquiries about your personal information, please contact us at pasha@cdcwinc.com.

Age Restrictions

Our website is intended for individuals who are at least 21 years old. By using the Website, you confirm that you are at least 21 years of age and comply with all applicable laws and regulations regarding the use and purchase of cannabis products.

Compliance with Applicable Laws

We adhere to the data protection laws and regulations of the NY laws concerning the operation of dispensaries and the sale of cannabis.

Changes to this Privacy Policy

We reserve the right to update or modify this Privacy Policy at any time. Any changes will be effective upon posting the revised Privacy Policy on our website. We encourage you to review this Privacy Policy periodically.

If you have any questions, concerns, or requests regarding this Privacy Policy or our data practices, please contact us at pasha@cdcwinc.com.
